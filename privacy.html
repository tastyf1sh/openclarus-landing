Privacy notice

Last updated: 12 October 2026

This notice explains how Clarus Hospitality Ltd handles personal information associated with visits to this public information website and enquiries about Clarus. It does not cover the separate Clarus restaurant application; that service requires its own privacy documentation.

Who we are

Clarus Hospitality Ltd, registered in England and Wales under company number 17502845. For privacy enquiries email hello@openclarus.com.

Information we receive

If you email us, we may receive your name, email address, organisation, message and any information you choose to include. The website currently links to email rather than collecting enquiries through a web form. Our hosting provider may process technical connection information needed to deliver and secure the website.

Why we use it

We use enquiry information to respond to requests, discuss potential early access and manage related business correspondence. For handling ordinary business enquiries, we rely on our legitimate interests in responding to people who contact us and administering those communications. We do not currently operate a newsletter or marketing mailing list, and we do not add you to one because you email us.

Who may receive it

We use IONOS for business email hosting and forwarding, and Cloudflare for website hosting, delivery and security. These providers may process personal data as needed to deliver their services. Their processing infrastructure and sub-processors may involve transfers outside the UK; where UK data protection law requires safeguards for restricted transfers, we rely on the applicable contractual and legal safeguards. We do not sell enquiry contact details.

How long we keep it

We normally retain general enquiry correspondence for 12 months after the last communication, unless a legal obligation, dispute or ongoing business matter requires a longer period. We periodically review and delete messages that are no longer needed. You can ask us about a particular enquiry using the contact address above.

Your rights

Subject to applicable law, you may request access to, correction or deletion of your personal data, object to certain processing, or request restriction. Contact us using the email above. You may also complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office.

Cookies and website technology

The current public website does not intentionally use analytics, advertising trackers or non-essential cookies. In a browser check on 12 October 2026, no cookies were observed for the website and no analytics requests were detected. Cloudflare may process IP addresses, request metadata and other technical information to deliver and protect the website, and may use essential security technologies where needed. If we introduce non-essential cookies or similar technologies, we will update this notice and obtain consent where required.

c/o Parker Cavendish, Suite 301, Stanmore Business and Innovation Centre, Howard Road, Stanmore, Middlesex, HA7 1FW, United Kingdom.

Changes

We will update this notice if our website, services or data-handling arrangements change.

Scope: This notice covers the public Clarus website and general enquiries only. The restaurant application has separate data-handling requirements and will require its own documentation.

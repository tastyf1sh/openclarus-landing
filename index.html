WHAT CLARUS BRINGS TOGETHER

Built for the whole
hospitality operation.

Not another isolated spreadsheet or single-purpose tool. Clarus brings purchasing, kitchen controls and commercial decisions into the same conversation.

01
KITCHEN & STOCK

Know what you've got. Know what it costs.

Keep ingredients and non-food stock organised, monitor supplier prices and make stock movement visible across the business.

  • Central stock catalogue with suppliers, categories, units and pack sizes
  • Supplier price tracking and comparisons
  • Resumable stocktakes, history and variance review
  • Multi-site stock transfers and wastage tracking
02
RECIPES & PROFITABILITY

Understand the margin behind every dish.

Take recipes beyond a list of ingredients: connect purchase costs to portions, batches, selling prices and menu performance.

  • Recipe, portion and batch costing
  • Dish-level food cost and gross profit
  • Printable recipe and service cards
  • Menu-section GP and prepared-item cost breakdowns
03
ALLERGENS & FOOD SAFETY

Practical tools for careful kitchen decisions.

Carry recorded ingredient information into chef-reviewed recipes and give teams clear reference documents for service.

  • Ingredient-linked allergen records and recipe controls
  • Customer allergen cards and menu builder
  • Daily specials sheets, matrices and training cards
  • Printable HACCP documentation from kitchen templates
These tools support professional verification and food-safety processes; they do not guarantee compliance or replace checks against supplier labels and specifications.
04
PURCHASING & INVOICES

From ordering to invoice review.

Bring supplier orders, invoice documents and product records into a more coherent purchasing workflow.

  • Supplier orders, drafts, purchase tasks and pre-orders
  • Invoice import with source-document review
  • Product matching using supplier and packaging information
  • Statement checking, invoice history and search
  • AI-assisted new-product suggestions in the V2 pilot, always editable and chef-reviewed
05
DAILY OPERATIONS

Keep the day's moving parts connected.

Understand demand and record the costs that don't always appear on the menu.

  • Food revenue and covers entry
  • Upcoming covers and Eat App-connected booking outlook
  • Breakfast allocations and staff meals
  • Function revenue, costs and margin tracking
  • Operational cost areas such as bakery or pizza
06
MANAGEMENT INSIGHT

See the operation — not just the totals.

Put useful commercial information in front of chefs, managers and owners, with visibility across restaurant sites.

  • Daily spend, revenue, covers, revenue per cover and GP
  • Supplier and category purchasing reports
  • Stock values, stocktake variance and transfers
  • Trends in purchasing and operating costs
  • Broader multi-site owner overview

Running hospitality without clear data is guesswork.

Invoices get buried, margins drift, and reporting often arrives too late to actually help. Clarus is built to turn that chaos into something structured and useful.

The problem

  • Invoices live in email inboxes, folders, and printed piles.
  • Stock, recipes, and allergens end up spread across different systems.
  • Gross profit issues get spotted too late to react properly.
  • Managers spend time chasing numbers instead of running service.

The Clarus way

  • Bring invoice, stock, costing, and reporting into one place.
  • See cleaner supplier spend, item costs, and margin performance.
  • Build recipe and allergen information around real operational use.
  • Make decisions from live data instead of end-of-month surprises.

Everything you need to understand what the kitchen is really doing.
